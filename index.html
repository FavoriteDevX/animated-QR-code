Under the hood

Why does it still scan?

🛡️

Level-H error correction

QR codes carry Reed–Solomon redundancy. At level H up to 30% of the code can be damaged, hidden or in motion and the data is still recovered perfectly.

🎯

Locked anchors

The three corner eyes and all alignment patterns are rendered static, full-size and full-contrast. Your phone locks on instantly, no matter what the data modules are doing.

📐

Scan-safe motion limits

Modules never shrink below 60% or drift more than a fifth of a module from their centre, and decorations only touch light areas at low opacity — so every sample point keeps its colour.

🔬

Verified, not promised

A real decoder runs on the live preview several times per second, and the scan audit decodes frames across the entire loop. If a setting hurts readability, you'll see it immediately.

FAQ

Good to know

Where can I use an animated QR code?

Anywhere a video or GIF can play: digital signage, event screens, livestream overlays, websites, social posts, presentations and email (GIF). For print, export a PNG frame — the static frame is fully scannable too.

Which export format should I choose?

WebM gives the smoothest, smallest video for screens and editors. GIF plays everywhere — chat apps, emails, slides — at the cost of a larger file. PNG captures the current frame in high resolution.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Encoding, rendering, verification and export all run locally in your browser. Logos you add never leave your device.

My code says "Weak" — what should I change?

Lower the intensity, raise error correction to H, increase colour contrast, keep the quiet zone at 4, or shorten the content (shorter content = bigger modules). The badge updates live as you tweak.

Do these codes expire?

Never. These are static QR codes: the content is encoded directly in the pattern, with no redirect service in between.
